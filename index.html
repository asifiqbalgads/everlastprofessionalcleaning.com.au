The Everlast Standard

Inside our room-by-room inspection checklist.

Whether preparing a rental property for final handover or booking a detailed residential reset, every surface, track, and fixture follows an accountable scope.

Custom Scope Options Need oven degreasing, exterior window tracks, wall washing, or yard & rubbish tidy-up? Bundle them cleanly into one quote.

General Interiors & Hard-to-Reach Details

11 checkpoints
  • All exhaust fans and air vents dusted and cleaned throughout
  • Flyscreens dusted free from dust and cobwebs
  • Interior window glass, sills, and accessible window tracking cleaned
  • Venetian blinds dusted carefully where applicable
  • Doors, door frames, and wardrobe mirror doors wiped spotless
  • Wall scuff marks removed (partial or full wall wash quoted if required)
  • Skirting boards, light switches, and power points wiped clean
  • All accessible light fittings and ceiling fans dusted
  • Cobwebs removed across ceilings, cornices, and hallways
  • Stairs, hallways, and living zones vacuumed and mopped
  • Balcony cleaning & rubbish removal available as add-ons

Kitchen, Cabinetry & Full Oven Clean

7 checkpoints
  • All cabinetry cleaned inside and out, including tops of cupboards
  • Drawers wiped clean and cleared of leftover debris or residue
  • Benchtops, tiled splashbacks, and surrounding walls degreased
  • Sink basin, drain, and tapware descaled and polished
  • Full oven clean including interior cavity, oven racks, and glass door
  • Rangehood exterior, canopy, stovetop, and removable filters cleaned
  • Dishwasher cleaned inside and out including bottom drainer filter

Bathrooms, Ensuite & Toilets

8 checkpoints
  • Vanity benchtops, basins, tapware, and cupboards cleaned inside and out
  • Mirrors cleaned streak-free
  • Glass shower screens treated to remove soap scum and water build-up
  • Showerhead, mixers, and chrome fittings polished
  • Tile grout scrubbed and mould build-up targeted where required
  • Shower base, bath tub, tapware, and bath drains thoroughly sanitised
  • Toilets disinfected top to base including hinges and cistern
  • Tiled floors vacuumed and sanitised

Bedrooms, Robes & Laundry

6 checkpoints
  • All bedroom floors vacuumed and mopped where hard-floored
  • Walk-in robes and built-in wardrobes cleaned inside including sliding tracks
  • Wardrobe mirrors, handles, and shelving wiped free of dust
  • Laundry trough, tapware, and plughole scrubbed and rinsed
  • Laundry cupboards cleaned inside and out
  • Dryer lint area and surrounding splashback surfaces wiped down

Windows, Glass & 5-Step Steam Extraction

8 checkpoints
  • Interior & exterior accessible window glass, frames, sills, and tracks
  • Glass sliding doors, entry glazing, and mirrors polished streak-free
  • Step 1 Steam: Carpet, upholstery, or mattress surface assessment
  • Step 2 Steam: Pre-treatment solution applied to loosen embedded grime
  • Step 3 Steam: Targeted spot & stain treatment (subject to fabric/age)
  • Step 4 Steam: Hot-water extraction removing dirt, odours, and moisture
  • Step 5 Steam: Final quality inspection across all treated rooms
  • Optional add-on to any End-of-Lease package to avoid extra bond callouts
